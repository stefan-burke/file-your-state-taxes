      # How can I access my 2023 state tax return?
  - type: markdown
    content: |-
      If you filed your 2023 New York state tax return with us last year we can help you get a copy of that return. To download your 2023 state tax return directly from FileYourStateTaxes, please [sign in here](https://www.fileyourstatetaxes.org/en/state_file/archived_intakes/email_address/edit).

      Otherwise, you can visit your state tax department’s website for instructions on how to request a copy of your 2023 state tax return from the department.

      New York filers can visit [this webpage](https://www.tax.ny.gov/help/contact/get-copy-of-return.htm#:~:text=To%20request%20copies%20of%20e,Returns%20and%2For%20Tax%20Information) to download Form DTF-505 and find instructions for mailing. Processing the request may take 30 days and will cost $0.25 per page.

      # How can I access my 2024 state tax return?
  - type: markdown
    content: |-
      If you filed your 2024 Maryland state tax return with FileYourStateTaxes we can help you get a copy. Log in to FileYourStateTaxes to download your tax return.

      Otherwise, you can visit your state tax department’s website for instructions on how to request a copy of your 2024 state tax return from the department.

      Maryland filers can [download Form 129](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/tax/forms/misc/129.pdf) and find instructions for mailing. Processing the request may take 15 business days and there is no charge for a copy of your return.

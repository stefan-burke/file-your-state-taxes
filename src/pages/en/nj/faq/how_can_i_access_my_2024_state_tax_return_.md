      # How can I access my 2024 state tax return?
  - type: markdown
    content: |-
      If you filed your 2024 New Jersey state tax return with FileYourStateTaxes we can help you get a copy. Log in to FileYourStateTaxes to download your tax return.

      Otherwise, you can visit your state tax department’s website for instructions on how to request a copy of your 2024 state tax return from the department.

      New Jersey filers can download [Form DCC-1](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/pdf/other_forms/misc/dcc.pdf) and find instructions for mailing. The New Jersey Division of Taxation will respond to your request within 30 business days.

      # How is my personal information handled?
  - type: markdown
    content: |-
      FileYourStateTaxes uses industry best practices to ensure your data is kept safe and follows the SOC 2 security standard.

      You may access your tax return for up to three years after its extended due date. After that date your information will be deleted from FileYourStateTaxes.

      If you wish to have your information deleted prior to that date from FileYourStateTaxes, please email us at help@fileyourstatetaxes.org.

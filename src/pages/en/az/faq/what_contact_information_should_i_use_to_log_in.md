      # What contact information should I use to log in?
  - type: markdown
    content: |-
      If you filed a 2023 tax return with us, you will need to provide the email address you used to create your FileYourStateTaxes account.

      If you filed a 2024 tax return with us, you will need to provide either the email address or phone number used to create your account.

      The account details you used to log in may be different from the way we contacted you. For example, the phone number or email address we used to send you updates may not be the same as the one you used to create your account.

      \*Note - If you filed both 2023 and 2024 with us, you likely used a phone number to create your 2024 account.

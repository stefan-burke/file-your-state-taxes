      # How can I access my 2023 and 2024 state tax return?
  - type: markdown
    content: |-
      If you filed your 2023 or 2024 Arizona state tax return with us we can help you get a copy of that return. To download your 2023 or 2024 state tax return directly from FileYourStateTaxes, please [sign in here](https://www.fileyourstatetaxes.org/en/state_file/archived_intakes/email_address/edit).

      Otherwise, you can visit your state tax department’s website for instructions on how to request a copy of your 2023 or 2024 state tax return from the department.

      Arizona filers can visit [this webpage](https://azdor.gov/forms/other-forms/request-copies-tax-documents) to download Form 450 and find instructions for mailing. Processing the request may take 10 days and cost up to $7.00.

      # How can I access my 2024 state tax return?
  - type: markdown
    content: |-
      If you filed your 2024 Idaho state tax return with FileYourStateTaxes we can help you get a copy. Log in to FileYourStateTaxes to download your tax return.

      Otherwise, you can visit your state tax department’s website for instructions on how to request a copy of your 2024 state tax return from the department.

      Idaho filers can visit [this webpage](https://tax.idaho.gov/guides/public-records-requests/) to download [Form STC-06](https://tax.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/forms/EFO00141/EFO00141_12-12-2022.pdf) and find instructions for mailing. Processing the request may take 10 business days and may require a payment depending on the size of the packet.

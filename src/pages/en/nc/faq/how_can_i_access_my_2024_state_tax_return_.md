      # How can I access my 2024 state tax return?
  - type: markdown
    content: |-
      If you filed your 2024 North Carolina state tax return with FileYourStateTaxes we can help you get a copy. Log in to FileYourStateTaxes to download your tax return.

      Otherwise, you can visit your state tax department’s website for instructions on how to request a copy of your 2024 state tax return from the department.

      North Carolina filers can visit [this webpage](https://www.ncdor.gov/taxes-forms/individual-income-tax/customer-education-individuals/how-get-copy-your-north-carolina-return) to find instructions for mailing a written request. Processing the request may take 20 days and there is no charge for a copy of your return.
